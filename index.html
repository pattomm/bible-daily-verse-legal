Privacy Policy

Eloa · Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Español · Português

Eloa ("the app", "we") is a Bible reading and spiritual companion app. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We keep it simple because our data practices are simple: we do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

1. Information we collect

Account information (optional). You can use the app without an account. If you create one, we collect your email address to identify your account and sync your content across devices. You also choose a password, but it is handled directly by our authentication provider (Supabase) over an encrypted connection and is stored only as a secure, one-way hash. Neither we nor our provider can see or recover your actual password.

Content you create. Highlights, bookmarks, written notes, voice notes (audio recordings you choose to record), saved guidance results, and your reading streak. Without an account, this content stays on your device; with an account, it is synced to our backend so you don't lose it.

Personalization answers. During onboarding you share your first name and may share your age range, denomination, how you're feeling, and your spiritual interests. These answers are used only to personalize your experience in the app (for example, greeting you by name). Every question except your first name can be skipped. Without an account they stay on your device; with an account they are stored in your profile.

Guidance requests. When you use Guidance with AI turned on, the emotion, intensity, and any context text you provide are processed to generate a scripture-based response. AI is used only with your permission (see "AI features" below).

Subscription status. Whether you have an active premium subscription, so the app can unlock premium features. Payments are processed entirely by Apple — we never see or store your payment card details.

What we do NOT collect: location, contacts, photos, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or any analytics/behavioral tracking data.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features: reading, notes, sync, daily phrases, widgets, and reminders.
  • To personalize your experience, such as greeting you by name and tailoring guidance to what you share.
  • To manage your subscription and unlock premium features.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we never sell it to anyone.

3. Services we rely on

The app uses a small number of service providers, strictly to operate its features:

  • Supabase — our backend (authentication, database, and storage). Your account and synced content are stored here. (Supabase privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. Receives an app user ID (anonymous, or your account ID if you are signed in) and purchase receipts from Apple to validate your subscription. (RevenueCat privacy policy)
  • OpenAI — powers the AI Guidance and daily content features. Only if you allow AI, when you request guidance the emotion, intensity, and text you wrote (never your name, email, or account details) are sent to OpenAI's API to generate a response. (OpenAI privacy policy)
  • Apple — processes all payments and manages your subscription billing through your Apple ID.

4. AI features

Guidance uses AI only with your explicit permission. The first time you request guidance, the app asks whether to allow AI and explains exactly what is sent: the emotion you choose, its intensity, and the optional text you write. Your name, email, notes, highlights, and account data are never sent.

  • If you allow it, those inputs are sent to our backend and forwarded to OpenAI to produce the response. They are not used to identify you and are not combined with your account profile.
  • If you decline, the app shows general guidance created on your device and sends nothing.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy → AI Guidance.

The daily verse explanation, reflection, and prayer are general content generated once per day per language. They contain no information about you.

5. Notifications

Daily reminders are optional and scheduled locally on your device, as is the reminder sent before a free trial ends. You can change or disable reminders anytime in the app's settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Content stored only on your device is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Synced content remains in your account until you delete it or delete your account.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app: Settings → Delete Account (shown while you are signed in). This permanently removes your account, notes, highlights, bookmarks, voice notes, and personalization data.
  • You can also request deletion by contacting us at the email below; we will complete the request within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Synced content is stored with our backend provider (Supabase) using industry-standard protections, and access to your synced content requires your authenticated account. Passwords are never stored in plain text: authentication is handled by Supabase, which keeps only a secure one-way hash of your password that cannot be reversed into the original.

To protect the AI features from abuse, our backend limits how many requests can be made. For this it keeps a keyed, irreversible hash of the requesting IP address (never the IP address itself) for up to 2 days, and then deletes it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, email us — we honor these requests for all users regardless of location.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
